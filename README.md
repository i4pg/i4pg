# Abdullah Al Attas

Senior Business Analyst in Riyadh. I look after the operational systems of a B2B wholesale group (ERP, master
data, warehouse and sales channels) and I stay hands-on: NetSuite, Python, and the small tools that hold
integrations together.

I trained as an industrial engineer and came to enterprise systems from the factory floor. I measure before I automate.

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/abdullah-al-attas-4385a42b5)

## What I work on

- **ERP and finance flows**: NetSuite design and SuiteScript, invoicing, customer statements, cash application
- **Integrations that don't fail silently**: idempotent writes, replay, webhook reliability
- **Warehouse and master data**: lot and expiry control, FEFO allocation, catalogue governance
